## Lecture Outline (Laplace Transform Property: Time Shifting)

### 1. Main Topic of the Lecture
- Introduction to two important Laplace transform properties:
  - **time shifting**
  - **time-derivative property**
- In this lecture, the worked example focuses on **time shifting**.

---

### 2. Time-Shifting Property of the Laplace Transform
- If:
  $$ x(t) \;\Longleftrightarrow\; X(s) $$
- Then for a right-sided / causal signal:
  $$ x(t-t_0)u(t-t_0) \;\Longleftrightarrow\; e^{-st_0}X(s) $$
- Interpretation:
  - delaying a signal by $t_0$
  - multiplies its Laplace transform by:
    $$ e^{-st_0} $$

---

### 3. Important Assumption
- The lecture assumes the signal is **right-sided**:
  $$ x(t)=0 \quad \text{for } t<0 $$
- That is why the shifted version is written with a unit step:
  $$ x(t-t_0)u(t-t_0) $$

---

### 4. Worked Example: Piecewise Signal
- A piecewise signal is given with:
  - a ramp from $t=1$ to $t=2$
  - a constant section from $t=2$ to $t=4$
- The signal is rewritten in terms of unit steps as:
  $$
  x(t) = (t-1)\big[u(t-1)-u(t-2)\big] + u(t-2)-u(t-4)
  $$

---

### 5. Expanding the Signal into Simpler Parts
- Distribute the first term:
  $$
  x(t) = (t-1)u(t-1) - (t-1)u(t-2) + u(t-2) - u(t-4)
  $$
- To handle the shifted ramp term, rewrite:
  $$
  (t-1)u(t-2) = \big[(t-2)+1\big]u(t-2)
  $$
  so:
  $$
  (t-1)u(t-2) = (t-2)u(t-2) + u(t-2)
  $$

---

### 6. Basic Laplace Transform Pairs Used
- Unit step:
  $$
  \mathcal{L}\{u(t)\} = \frac{1}{s}
  $$
- Shifted unit step:
  $$
  \mathcal{L}\{u(t-a)\} = \frac{e^{-as}}{s}
  $$
- Ramp:
  $$
  \mathcal{L}\{t\,u(t)\} = \frac{1}{s^2}
  $$
- Shifted ramp:
  $$
  \mathcal{L}\{(t-a)u(t-a)\} = \frac{e^{-as}}{s^2}
  $$

---

### 7. Transform of Each Part
- For the first shifted ramp:
  $$
  \mathcal{L}\{(t-1)u(t-1)\} = \frac{e^{-s}}{s^2}
  $$
- For the second shifted ramp term:
  $$
  \mathcal{L}\{(t-2)u(t-2)\} = \frac{e^{-2s}}{s^2}
  $$
- For the shifted step terms:
  $$
  \mathcal{L}\{u(t-2)\} = \frac{e^{-2s}}{s}
  $$
  $$
  \mathcal{L}\{u(t-4)\} = \frac{e^{-4s}}{s}
  $$

---

### 8. Combine the Results
- Substitute the transforms into the expanded expression:
  $$
  X(s) = \frac{e^{-s}}{s^2}
  - \left(\frac{e^{-2s}}{s^2} + \frac{e^{-2s}}{s}\right)
  + \frac{e^{-2s}}{s}
  - \frac{e^{-4s}}{s}
  $$
- Simplify:
  $$
  X(s) = \frac{e^{-s}-e^{-2s}}{s^2} - \frac{e^{-4s}}{s}
  $$

---

### 9. Main Strategy Highlighted in the Lecture
- To find the Laplace transform of a piecewise signal:
  1. express the signal using shifted unit-step functions
  2. rewrite shifted ramp terms into standard shifted forms
  3. apply the time-shifting property
  4. add the transformed pieces together
