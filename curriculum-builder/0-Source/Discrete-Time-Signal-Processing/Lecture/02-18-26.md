## Lecture Outline (Feedback Systems + Ideal LP/HP Filter Impulse Responses)

### 1. Frequency-Domain View of LTI Systems
- Convolution in time:
  $$ y[n] = x[n] * h[n] $$
- Multiplication in frequency:
  $$ Y(e^{j\omega}) = X(e^{j\omega})H(e^{j\omega}) $$

---

### 2. Feedback Interconnection (Closed-Loop Transfer Function)
- Define intermediate signals in the block diagram:
  - Forward path: $H_1(e^{j\omega})$
  - Feedback path: $H_2(e^{j\omega})$
- Relationships (from the diagram algebra):
  $$ Y(e^{j\omega}) = V(e^{j\omega})H_1(e^{j\omega}) $$
  $$ W(e^{j\omega}) = Y(e^{j\omega})H_2(e^{j\omega}) $$
  $$ V(e^{j\omega}) = X(e^{j\omega}) - W(e^{j\omega}) $$
- Substitute to solve for the overall transfer function:
  $$
  Y(e^{j\omega})\big[1 + H_1(e^{j\omega})H_2(e^{j\omega})\big]
  = X(e^{j\omega})H_1(e^{j\omega})
  $$
- Closed-loop frequency response:
  $$ H(e^{j\omega}) = \frac{Y(e^{j\omega})}{X(e^{j\omega})}
  = \frac{H_1(e^{j\omega})}{1 + H_1(e^{j\omega})H_2(e^{j\omega})} $$

---

### 3. Deriving $Y(e^{j\omega}) = H(e^{j\omega})X(e^{j\omega})$ from Convolution
- Start with:
  $$ y[n] = \sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty} h[m]\,x[n-m] $$
- Take the DTFT and rearrange:
  $$ Y(e^{j\omega}) = \left(\sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty} h[m]e^{-j\omega m}\right)X(e^{j\omega}) $$
- Identify:
  $$ H(e^{j\omega}) = \sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty} h[m]e^{-j\omega m} $$

---

### 4. Ideal Lowpass Filter in Frequency Domain
- Rectangular frequency response:
  $$
  H_{LP,\text{ideal}}(e^{j\omega}) =
  \begin{cases}
  1, & |\omega|\le \omega_c \\
  0, & \text{otherwise}
  \end{cases}
  $$

---

### 5. Ideal Lowpass Impulse Response (Inverse DTFT)
- Inverse DTFT:
  $$ h_{LP,\text{ideal}}[n] = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\omega_c}^{\omega_c} e^{j\omega n}\,d\omega $$
- Results:
  - For $n=0$:
    $$ h_{LP,\text{ideal}}[0] = \frac{\omega_c}{\pi} $$
  - For $n\ne 0$:
    $$ h_{LP,\text{ideal}}[n] = \frac{\sin(\omega_c n)}{\pi n} $$
- Sinc-style rewrite:
  $$ h_{LP,\text{ideal}}[n] = \frac{\omega_c}{\pi}\,\frac{\sin(\omega_c n)}{\omega_c n} $$

---

### 6. Truncation / Windowing the Ideal Lowpass
- Truncate the infinite sinc to a finite interval:
  $$ h_{LP,\text{trunc}}[n] = h_{LP,\text{ideal}}[n]\;w[n] $$
- Rectangular window example:
  $$
  w[n] =
  \begin{cases}
  1, & -N \le n \le N \\
  0, & \text{otherwise}
  \end{cases}
  $$

---

### 7. Ideal Highpass Filter via Complement of Lowpass
- Ideal highpass frequency response:
  $$
  H_{HP,\text{ideal}}(e^{j\omega}) =
  \begin{cases}
  1, & \omega_c \le |\omega| \le \pi \\
  0, & |\omega| < \omega_c
  \end{cases}
  $$
- Inverse DTFT setup:
  $$ h_{HP,\text{ideal}}[n] = \frac{1}{2\pi}\left(\int_{-\pi}^{-\omega_c} e^{j\omega n}d\omega
  + \int_{\omega_c}^{\pi} e^{j\omega n}d\omega\right) $$

---

### 8. Ideal Highpass Impulse Response (Key Result)
- Relationship to lowpass using $\delta[n]$:
  $$ h_{HP,\text{ideal}}[n] = \delta[n] - h_{LP,\text{ideal}}[n] $$
- Therefore:
  - For $n=0$:
    $$ h_{HP,\text{ideal}}[0] = 1 - \frac{\omega_c}{\pi} = \frac{\pi-\omega_c}{\pi} $$
  - For $n\ne 0$:
    $$ h_{HP,\text{ideal}}[n] = -\frac{\sin(\omega_c n)}{\pi n} $$

---

### 9. Frequency Shifting by Modulation (Nyquist Shift)
- Multiply in time by $e^{j\pi n}$ to shift frequency by $\pi$:
  $$ \tilde{h}_{LP}[n] = h_{LP}[n]e^{j\pi n} $$
- DTFT shift property (conceptually):
  $$ \tilde{H}(e^{j\omega}) = H(e^{j(\omega-\pi)}) $$
