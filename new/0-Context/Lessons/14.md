## Finding the Square Root of a Negative Number

**Example:** Evaluate $\sqrt{-36}$.

**Explanation**

The imaginary unit $i$ is defined as

$$
i = \sqrt{-1}
$$

Therefore, the given number can be rewritten as

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sqrt{-36}
&= \sqrt{36 \cdot (-1)} \\
&= \sqrt{36} \cdot \sqrt{-1} \\
&= 6i
\end{aligned}
$$

## Finding the Negative Square Root of a Negative Number

**Example:** Find the value of $-\sqrt{-9}$.

**Explanation**

The imaginary unit $i$ is defined as

$$
i = \sqrt{-1}
$$

Therefore, the given number can be rewritten as

$$
\begin{aligned}
-\sqrt{-9}
&= -\sqrt{9 \cdot (-1)} \\
&= -\sqrt{9} \cdot \sqrt{-1} \\
&= -3i
\end{aligned}
$$
