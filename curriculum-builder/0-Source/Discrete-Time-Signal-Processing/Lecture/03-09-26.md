## Lecture Outline (DFS, DTFT Sampling, and Periodic Signals)

### 1. Discrete Fourier Series (DFS) Representation
- For an $N$-periodic signal:
  $$ x[n] = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{k=0}^{N-1} X(k)\,e^{j\frac{2\pi}{N}kn} $$
- DFS coefficients:
  $$ X(k) = \sum_{n=0}^{N-1} x[n]e^{-j\frac{2\pi}{N}kn} $$
- Interpretation:
  - Signal expressed as sum of complex exponentials
  - Fundamental frequency:
    $$ \frac{2\pi}{N} $$

---

### 2. Harmonic Interpretation
- Any periodic signal with period $N$ can be written as:
  - Sum of harmonics at:
    $$ \omega_k = \frac{2\pi k}{N}, \quad k=0,1,\dots,N-1 $$
- Each term:
  $$ e^{j\omega_k n} $$
- Coefficients $X(k)$ determine amplitude and phase

---

### 3. Relationship Between DFS and DTFT
- DTFT:
  $$ X(e^{j\omega}) = \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} x[n]e^{-j\omega n} $$
- DFS coefficients are samples of the DTFT:
  $$ X(k) = X(e^{j\omega})\big|_{\omega=\frac{2\pi k}{N}} $$
- Frequency spacing:
  $$ \Delta\omega = \frac{2\pi}{N} $$

---

### 4. Reconstruction from Samples (Riemann Sum View)
- DFS reconstruction:
  $$ x[n] = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{k=0}^{N-1} X(k)e^{j\frac{2\pi}{N}kn} $$
- Rewrite:
  $$ x[n] = \frac{\Delta\omega}{2\pi}\sum_{k=0}^{N-1} X(e^{jk\Delta\omega})e^{jk\Delta\omega n} $$
- As $N \to \infty$:
  - $\Delta\omega \to d\omega$
  - Sum becomes integral:
  $$ x[n] = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{0}^{2\pi} X(e^{j\omega})e^{j\omega n}\,d\omega $$

---

### 5. Example: Periodic Rectangular Sequence
- Signal:
  $$
  x[n] =
  \begin{cases}
  1, & |n|\le M \\
  0, & \text{otherwise}
  \end{cases}
  \quad \text{(period } N)
  $$
- DFS coefficients:
  $$ X(k) = \sum_{n=-M}^{M} e^{-j\frac{2\pi}{N}kn} $$

---

### 6. Evaluating the Sum (Geometric Series)
- Rewrite:
  $$ X(k) = e^{j\frac{2\pi}{N}kM}\sum_{n=0}^{2M} e^{-j\frac{2\pi}{N}kn} $$
- Use geometric series formula:
  $$ \sum_{n=0}^{L} \alpha^n = \frac{1-\alpha^{L+1}}{1-\alpha} $$

---

### 7. Closed-Form Result (Dirichlet Kernel Form)
- Final result:
  $$ X(k) =
  \frac{\sin\!\left(\frac{2\pi k}{N}\frac{2M+1}{2}\right)}
       {\sin\!\left(\frac{2\pi k}{N}\frac{1}{2}\right)} $$
- Equivalent DTFT form:
  $$ X(e^{j\omega}) =
  \frac{\sin\!\left(\frac{\omega}{2}(2M+1)\right)}
       {\sin\!\left(\frac{\omega}{2}\right)} $$

---

### 8. Key Insight (Sampling Relationship)
- DFS samples the DTFT:
  $$ X(k) = X(e^{j\omega}) \text{ at } \omega=\frac{2\pi k}{N} $$
- Visualization:
  - Continuous DTFT curve
  - Discrete samples at equally spaced frequencies

---

### 9. Connection Between Continuous and Discrete Frequency Representations
- Continuous frequency variable:
  $$ \omega $$
- Discrete frequencies:
  $$ \omega_k = k\Delta\omega, \quad \Delta\omega = \frac{2\pi}{N} $$
- As $N \to \infty$:
  - DFS → DTFT
  - Sum → integral
