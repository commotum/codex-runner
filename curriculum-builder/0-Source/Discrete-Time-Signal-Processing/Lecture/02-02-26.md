## Lecture Outline (Convolution and Frequency Response)

### 1. Exam Review Problem – Windowed Exponential
- Given:
  $$ x[n] = 2^{-(n-3)}\left(u[n-3] - u[n-6]\right) $$
- Support:
  $$
  x[n] =
  \begin{cases}
  0, & n<3 \\
  2^{-(n-3)}, & 3 \le n \le 5 \\
  0, & n \ge 6
  \end{cases}
  $$
- Impulse representation:
  $$ x[n] = 1\,\delta[n-3] + \frac{1}{2}\,\delta[n-4] + \frac{1}{4}\,\delta[n-5] $$

---

### 2. Combining Sinusoids into Amplitude–Phase Form
- Given:
  $$ x[n] = 4\cos(0.3\pi n) + 3\sin(0.3\pi n) $$
- Convert sine to cosine:
  $$ 3\sin(\omega n) = 3\cos(\omega n - \tfrac{\pi}{2}) $$
- Express using complex exponentials:
  $$ x[n] = \Re\left\{ e^{j0.3\pi n}\left(4 + 3e^{-j\pi/2}\right) \right\} $$
- Combine phasors:
  $$ 4 + 3e^{-j\pi/2} = 4 - 3j = 5e^{j\theta} $$
- Final amplitude–phase form:
  $$ x[n] = 5\cos(0.3\pi n + \theta) $$

---

### 3. Convolution for LTI Systems
- Every LTI system:
  $$ y[n] = \sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty} h[m]\,x[n-m] $$
- Equivalent form:
  $$ y[n] = \sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty} x[m]\,h[n-m] $$

---

### 4. Graphical Convolution Procedure
To compute $y[n]$:
1. Reflect $h[m] \rightarrow h[-m]$
2. Shift to get $h[n-m]$
3. Multiply:
   $$ x[m]\,h[n-m] $$
4. Sum over $m$

---

### 5. FIR (Finite Impulse Response) Systems
- If:
  $$ h[m] \neq 0 \quad \text{only for } 0 \le m \le N-1 $$
- Then convolution reduces to:
  $$ y[n] = \sum_{m=0}^{N-1} x[n-m]\,h[m] $$

---

### 6. Frequency Response of LTI Systems
- Input:
  $$ x[n] = e^{j\omega_0 n} $$
- Output:
  $$ y[n] = \sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty} h[m]\,e^{j\omega_0(n-m)} $$
- Factor:
  $$ y[n] = e^{j\omega_0 n}
     \sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty} h[m] e^{-j\omega_0 m} $$
- Define frequency response:
  $$ H(e^{j\omega}) =
     \sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty} h[m] e^{-j\omega m} $$

---

### 7. Interpretation of Frequency Response
- Output for complex exponential input:
  $$ y[n] = H(e^{j\omega_0})\,e^{j\omega_0 n} $$
- Magnitude–phase form:
  $$ H(e^{j\omega}) =
     |H(e^{j\omega})|\,e^{j\theta(\omega)} $$
- Magnitude response:
  $$ |H(e^{j\omega})| $$
- Phase response:
  $$ \theta(\omega) $$

---

### 8. Response to Cosine Input
- Using:
  $$ \cos(\omega_0 n)
     = \frac{e^{j\omega_0 n} + e^{-j\omega_0 n}}{2} $$
- Output:
  $$
  y[n]
  = |H(e^{j\omega_0})|
    \cos\big(\omega_0 n + \theta(\omega_0)\big)
  $$

---

### 9. Conjugate Symmetry (Real Impulse Response)
- If $h[n]$ is real:
  $$ H^*(e^{j\omega}) = H(e^{-j\omega}) $$
- Leads to:
  - Even magnitude response
  - Odd phase response
