## Lecture Outline

### 1. Complex Exponentials and Polar Form

- Euler’s formula:
  $$ e^{j\theta} = \cos(\theta) + j\sin(\theta) $$
- Representation of complex numbers in polar form: $re^{j\theta}$
- Geometric interpretation of magnitude $r$ and angle $\theta$ in the complex plane

---

### 2. Geometric Series

- Finite geometric series:
  $$ S = 1 + x + x^2 + \dots + x^N $$
- Algebraic derivation of the closed form:
  $$ S = \frac{1 - x^{N+1}}{1 - x} $$
- Infinite series limit as $N \to \infty$ for $|x| < 1$:
  $$ \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} x^n = \frac{1}{1-x} $$
- Use of summation notation:
  $$ \sum_{n=0}^{N} x^n $$
- Index shifting and scaled series (e.g., $x^k \sum x^n$)

### 4. Mortgage / Loan as a Discrete-Time System

- Definitions:
  - Principal: $P_0$
  - Annual interest rate: $R$
  - Monthly rate: $r = \frac{R}{1200}$
  - Monthly payment: $M$
  - Number of payments: $N$
- Recursive system equation:
  $$ P[n] = P[n-1](1+r) - M $$
- Expansion of recursion over multiple steps
- Use of geometric series to sum interest terms
- Closed-form solution for monthly payment:
  $$ M = P_0 \frac{(1+r)^N r}{(1+r)^N - 1} $$
- Comparison of total payments for different loan lengths
- System interpretation:
  - Input: $M$
  - Output: $P[n]$

---

### 5. Exponential Functions and Power Series

- Definition of the exponential function $e^x$
- Maclaurin series expansion:
  $$ e^x = 1 + \frac{x}{1!} + \frac{x^2}{2!} + \frac{x^3}{3!} + \dots $$
- Term-by-term differentiation:
  $$ \frac{d}{dx} e^x = e^x $$
- Exponential identity:
  $$ e^x e^y = e^{x+y} $$
