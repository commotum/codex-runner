## Lecture Outline (DTFT Properties Continued)

### 1. DTFT Pair Reminder
- Forward transform:
  $$ X(e^{j\omega}) = \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} x[n]e^{-j\omega n} $$
- Inverse transform:
  $$ x[n] = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} X(e^{j\omega})e^{j\omega n}\,d\omega $$

---

### 2. Periodicity of the DTFT
- The DTFT is periodic in frequency:
  $$ X(e^{j(\omega+2\pi)}) = X(e^{j\omega}) $$
- Fundamental frequency interval:
  $$ -\pi \le \omega \le \pi $$

---

### 3. Time Shift Property
- If:
  $$ x[n] \;\Longleftrightarrow\; X(e^{j\omega}) $$
- Then:
  $$ x[n-n_0] \;\Longleftrightarrow\; e^{-j\omega n_0}X(e^{j\omega}) $$

---

### 4. Frequency Shift (Modulation) Property
- Multiplication by a complex exponential in time:
  $$ x[n]e^{j\omega_0 n} $$
- Corresponding frequency shift:
  $$ x[n]e^{j\omega_0 n} \;\Longleftrightarrow\; X(e^{j(\omega-\omega_0)}) $$

---

### 5. Time Reversal Property
- If:
  $$ x[n] \;\Longleftrightarrow\; X(e^{j\omega}) $$
- Then:
  $$ x[-n] \;\Longleftrightarrow\; X(e^{-j\omega}) $$

---

### 6. Conjugation Property
- If:
  $$ x[n] \;\Longleftrightarrow\; X(e^{j\omega}) $$
- Then:
  $$ x^*[n] \;\Longleftrightarrow\; X^*(e^{-j\omega}) $$

---

### 7. Symmetry for Real Signals
If $x[n]$ is real-valued:

- Conjugate symmetry:
  $$ X(e^{j\omega}) = X^*(e^{-j\omega}) $$

- Real part:
  $$ \Re\{X(e^{j\omega})\} \text{ is even in } \omega $$

- Imaginary part:
  $$ \Im\{X(e^{j\omega})\} \text{ is odd in } \omega $$

---

### 8. Even/Odd Time-Domain Signals
- If $x[n]$ is **even**:
  $$ x[n] = x[-n] $$
  Then:
  - $X(e^{j\omega})$ is real and even.

- If $x[n]$ is **odd**:
  $$ x[n] = -x[-n] $$
  Then:
  - $X(e^{j\omega})$ is imaginary and odd.

---

### 9. Differentiation Property
- Multiply by $n$ in time domain:
  $$ n\,x[n] $$
- Corresponding frequency-domain operation:
  $$ n\,x[n] \;\Longleftrightarrow\; j\frac{d}{d\omega}X(e^{j\omega}) $$

---

### 10. Convolution Property
- Convolution in time domain:
  $$ y[n] = x[n]*h[n] $$
- Corresponds to multiplication in frequency:
  $$ Y(e^{j\omega}) = X(e^{j\omega})H(e^{j\omega}) $$

---

### 11. Multiplication Property
- Multiplication in time:
  $$ y[n] = x[n]h[n] $$
- Corresponds to periodic convolution in frequency:
  $$
  Y(e^{j\omega}) =
  \frac{1}{2\pi}
  \int_{-\pi}^{\pi}
  X(e^{j\theta})H(e^{j(\omega-\theta)})\,d\theta
  $$
